G GateReady / Decision rulesSlide 3 · insight to action

Make arrival the outcome.

Keep early signals separate from mature outcomes; act only when verified-arrival evidence and capacity controls support it.

Leading signals: eligible roster, delivered reminders, identified→registered, confirmed count. Mature outcomes: unique admitted + verified external arrivals, incremental arrivals vs holdout, cost per incremental arrival.
